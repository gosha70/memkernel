# Origin note: the third review's corrected pass (2026-11-03)

As pasted by the owner on 2026-11-03, superseding "my prior two-finding review" (that earlier text never reached
this session). The earlier five findings stay addressed and the two scope decisions defensible. Its findings:

1. [P1] Imports lack cross-process atomicity: two wikis can adopt one legacy `ref_id`; two ungoverned imports can
   create duplicates. Require a project-wide cross-process import lock on both paths, or atomic claims. Test
   concurrent adoption and concurrent same-wiki import.
2. [P1] An interrupted supersession leaves two owned records that the duplicate rule refuses forever. Specify
   recovery from durable data; test a failure right after the replacement is created, governed and ungoverned.
3. [P1] `set_wiki_identity` can lose a concurrent tag update: metadata updates are fetch-and-merge and `tags` is
   one value. Require compare-and-set/retry or per-record serialization.
4. [P2] D4/D5 duplicates: duplicate predecessors, successors or deprecated-page records must be `ambiguous` with no
   store or ledger mutation, with tests for all three.

The spec answers them in S3 (recovery, D4/D5), S8 (import lock, metadata lock, `merge_tags`), FR-003a, FR-008,
SC-7 and SC-8.
