1. [P1] Path-only dedup recreates cross-wiki merging after a move: a new wiki at the old path can dedup into the
   moved wiki's record, and a moved wiki's frontmatter-only revision misses dedup. Dedup must use the full
   membership predicate, preferably `wiki-id`.
2. [P1] A matching `page_sha` proves revision content, not which wiki owns the record.
3. [P1] "No other wiki is known" is absence of evidence. Fail safely without durable ownership evidence, add an
   explicit owner-approved legacy-adoption mode, or weaken US1; a CHANGELOG disclosure doesn't satisfy US1.
4. [P1] A manifestless wiki can claim a manifested wiki's records: replacing the directory is indistinguishable
   from removing the manifest. Require durable ledger evidence or report ambiguity.
5. [P2] The two-ledger state has no safe behavior. Define reconciliation or fail closed before any store
   mutation; test add, remove, re-add; target already present; failed migration.
6. [P2] Directory movement is not an acceptance requirement. Add unchanged, frontmatter-only and old-path-reuse
   cases.

Also: replace "no wiki tag"/"untagged" with "carries neither `wiki-path:` nor `wiki-id:`", because 0.6 memories
carry the generic `wiki` tag.
