# Origin note: re-pin the `wiki_studio` arm to wiki-studio 0.4.1 (2026-10-18)

Decision by the repository owner on 2026-10-18, before any calibration or
measured session, following the order agreed with the wiki-studio reviewer:
release wiki-studio 0.4.1, move memkernel's tool pin and the arm-source tag to
it, build and verify task 17a against that exact version, then freeze the
protocol and run. No calibration or measured run starts until the re-pin is
merged, and the version does not change during the measured run.

Why. wiki-studio 0.4.1 (tag `v0.4.1`, commit
`8b5c8afe2b8f6a8a766596a3ed9729933f9c0e83`, on PyPI 2026-10-18) fixes
promote's rollback when a write fails part-way (gosha70/wiki-studio#21), so
a failed write during a measured run cannot leave the shared wiki half
written. It also fixes #23 and #24, neither on the arm's normal path.

What moves and what does not. At `v0.4.0` and `v0.4.1`,
`docs/agent-arm/instruction.md` (sha256 `c231bb40…`) and
`docs/agent-arm/config.frozen.json` (sha256 `51c4b11e…`) are byte-identical;
only the agent-arm README's pin line changed. The arm's instruction and its
upstream keys stay as they are; its CLI pin, its source tag and commit, the
calibration image's wiki-studio and the development lock move to 0.4.1.

Out of scope: removing FR-007's snapshot workaround (it exists only because
of #21) with the floor raised to 0.4.1 is a separate change after the
measured run, never while the protocol is frozen.
